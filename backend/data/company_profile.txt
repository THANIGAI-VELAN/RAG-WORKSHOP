ASTERION WORKS — COMPANY PROFILE

Asterion Works is a fictional employee-owned technology company headquartered at 480 Harbor Street, Portland, Oregon. It designs compact energy-monitoring and building-automation equipment for small offices, libraries, and community facilities. The company was founded in 2016 by electrical engineer Mara Chen and software engineer Luis Ortega after they built a low-cost power monitor for a neighborhood library.

The first product, the WattWatch One, shipped in 2017. In 2020 the company moved its assembly and support operations to Portland and adopted an employee-ownership trust. As of January 2025, Asterion Works has 86 employees: 54 in Portland, 18 remote elsewhere in the United States, and 14 in customer operations and field service. Its fiscal year runs from January 1 through December 31.

The company mission is to make building energy use understandable and actionable without requiring a dedicated facilities engineering team. Asterion sells directly through asterion.example and through a small network of authorized resellers in the United States. The company does not currently sell or ship outside the United States.

Customers can contact the support team at help@asterion.example or by phone at 1-800-555-0148, Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Pacific Time, excluding US federal holidays. General inquiries are normally acknowledged within one business day. Product troubleshooting and warranty claims should include the device serial number and order number when available.

Company office hours are 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Pacific Time, Monday through Friday. Employees may have different individual schedules under the employee handbook. The Portland office is closed on US federal holidays and the annual company planning day on the first Friday in October.
