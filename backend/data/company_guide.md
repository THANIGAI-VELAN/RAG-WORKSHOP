# Asterion Works Reference Guide

This fictional company reference collection is organized by topic:

- `company_profile.txt` is the primary source for company history, location, size, mission, and general office or support hours.
- `employee_policies.txt` is the primary source for employee schedules, attendance, paid time off, leave, holidays, and benefits. Its policy edition is January 2025.
- `products_and_support.txt` is the primary source for product features, US list prices, return terms, warranty coverage, customer support, and device troubleshooting. Its price list is effective February 1, 2025.
- `it_security.txt` is the primary source for account protection, device requirements, phishing, security incident reporting, and employee IT troubleshooting. Its policy edition is January 2025.

Use the topic-specific document when answering a detailed question. When the documents do not contain a requested fact, say it is not documented rather than extrapolating from a similar policy or product. Dates in a source describe the edition or effective period stated there; they are not promises that terms have remained unchanged after that date.
