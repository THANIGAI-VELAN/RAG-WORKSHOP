ASTERION WORKS — EMPLOYEE POLICIES
Policy edition: January 2025

WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE
The standard full-time schedule is 40 hours per week. The Portland office is generally open from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Pacific Time, Monday through Friday. With manager approval, employees may shift their start time between 7:00 a.m. and 10:00 a.m. Pacific Time, provided they work their agreed schedule and are available for their team's core collaboration hours of 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. Pacific Time. Remote employees follow the schedule agreed with their manager and must make meeting availability clear on the team calendar.

Employees record working time in the company timekeeping system by the end of each workday. If an employee will be late or absent, they should notify their manager and team channel as soon as practical, preferably before their scheduled start time. A missed time entry should be corrected by the end of the following business day. Repeated unreported absences are referred to People Operations for review; a single emergency absence is not treated as misconduct when the employee communicates as soon as possible.

PAID TIME OFF AND LEAVE
Full-time employees accrue 15 days (120 hours) of paid vacation per year during their first three years of service, increasing to 20 days (160 hours) per year beginning on the employee's third anniversary. Accrual is prorated during the first year. Vacation requests of one or two days should normally be submitted at least five business days in advance; requests of three or more consecutive workdays should normally be submitted at least two weeks in advance. Manager approval is required and depends on coverage. Employees may carry over up to 40 hours of unused vacation into the next calendar year. Hours above that limit are forfeited at year end unless local law requires otherwise.

Employees receive 10 paid sick days (80 hours) per calendar year. Sick time may be used for the employee's illness, preventive care, or qualifying care for a family member. Employees should notify their manager as soon as practical; advance notice is not required for an emergency. Unused sick time does not carry over and is not paid out when employment ends, except where required by law.

The company observes 11 paid holidays each year: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Indigenous Peoples' Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Day, the Friday after Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day. If a holiday falls on a weekend, People Operations publishes the observed weekday at the start of the year.

For parental leave, an employee who has completed 12 months of service may take up to 12 weeks of job-protected company leave following birth, adoption, or foster placement. The first six weeks are paid at 100% of base salary; the remaining six weeks are unpaid unless the employee elects to use accrued vacation or receives a statutory benefit. Employees should contact People Operations at least 30 days before a foreseeable leave. Applicable law may provide additional rights, and the more protective legal requirement applies.

BENEFITS AND REQUESTS
Full-time employees may enroll in the company medical, dental, and vision plans beginning the first day of the month after hire. Asterion contributes 80% of the employee-only medical premium; dependent coverage is available at the employee's cost. The company matches employee retirement-plan contributions dollar-for-dollar up to 4% of eligible pay after 90 days of employment.

Leave requests and policy questions go to the employee's manager or People Operations at people@asterion.example. The handbook is reviewed annually. This January 2025 policy edition is authoritative for the facts in this document; an individual employment agreement or applicable law may supersede a general policy.
